Escrow that follows the parcel.

VeriPay holds the buyer's funds in a NEAR smart contract and releases them along an agreed path: the logistics provider is paid when carrier pickup is verified, the seller is paid when delivery is verified by OutLayer's TEE and the buyer confirms receipt, and the platform fee is disclosed before funding.

1 · Agree

Seller creates the agreement: product, price, logistics fee, platform fee, deadlines.

2 · Fund

Buyer accepts and funds the exact total in tUSD — held by the contract, not by us.

3 · Settle

Carrier pickup verified → logistics paid. Delivery verified + buyer confirms → seller & fee paid.
