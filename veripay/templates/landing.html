How a trade runs

1 · Agree

Seller creates the agreement: product, price, logistics fee, platform fee, deadlines.

2 · Fund

Buyer accepts and funds the exact total in tUSD — held by the contract, not by us.

3 · Ship & verify

Logistics provider ships; OutLayer's TEE verifies the carrier report on-chain.

4 · Settle

Verified pickup pays logistics. Delivery + buyer confirmation pays seller and platform fee.
